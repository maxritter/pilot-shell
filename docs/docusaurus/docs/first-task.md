Pick a medium-sized feature in a repository you know. Put the App on the left and your agent's terminal on the right: your agent asks, and the App shows what each question is about.

1. **Describe it.** `/ql add a CSV export to the reports page` (`$ql` in Codex).
2. **Answer its questions.** Your agent reads the code and asks one question at a time, each with its recommendation.
3. **Approve the Plan.** It walks you through each decision with its diagram or mockup, then asks "Approve the Plan?".
4. **Start Implement.** The App shows one command to copy into a terminal.
5. **Agents build and check.** Nothing waits for you. You watch the slices fill in.
6. **Settle what is left.** Confirm what only you can, and look at the result.
7. **Approve the change.** Your agent offers to open the pull request.
