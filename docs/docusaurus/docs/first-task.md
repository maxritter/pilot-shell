Pick a medium-sized feature in a repository you know. Your agent asks each decision in the App, and its terminal shows a single line while it waits. Keep the App open beside your agent.

1. **Describe it.** Click **+ New** in the App, say what you want and copy the command it gives you. Or type `/ql add a CSV export to the reports page` in your agent (`$ql` in Codex).
2. **Answer its questions.** Your agent reads the code and groups independent questions into a batch in **Your turn**. Answer in any order; each answer goes to the agent immediately. Click a choice, type your own answer, or ask it to tell you more. **Undo** is available for five seconds.
3. **Approve the Plan.** Read it full size and comment on its diagrams and design. Agree to each **Done means** point, settle the decisions in **Your turn**, then approve the Plan.
4. **Start Implement.** Implement opens with your Build defaults and one command to copy.
5. **Agents build and check.** Your agent works on its own until the final review. You watch the slices fill in.
6. **Settle what is left.** Confirm what only you can, and look at the result.
7. **Approve the change.** Approve, and the pull request opens with its proof.
