title: The App

Your agent asks every decision in its own window. The QualityLayer App beside it is where you read and look: the Plan and its diagrams, the result, the proof. Put it on the left and your agent on the right. The App never starts or stops an agent; close it any time and your agents keep working.

It runs on macOS, Windows and Linux. Without a screen, `qualitylayer app` opens it in your browser.

## One layout for every step

![The App with numbered parts: the sidebar, the step tabs, the step line, the items that need you, the line of what agents checked, and the work](pathname:///img/diagrams/app-marked-light.svg)
![The App with numbered parts: the sidebar, the step tabs, the step line, the items that need you, the line of what agents checked, and the work](pathname:///img/diagrams/app-marked-dark.svg)

| Part | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **1** Sidebar | Your tasks: **Needs you**, **Running** and **Shipped** |
| **2** Step tabs | Where the task stands; an amber number means items wait for you |
| **3** Step line | Whose turn it is, in one sentence |
| **4** Needs you | Only what needs a person, shown as the thing itself |
| **5** Checked by agents | One violet line with what agents proved; it opens to the evidence |
| **6** The work | The document, the live build or the diff |

## Items and their answers {#items}

![The five families of items: Decide, Look, Confirm, Fix and Answer, each with its answers and the kinds of item in it](pathname:///img/diagrams/items-light.svg)
![The five families of items: Decide, Look, Confirm, Fix and Answer, each with its answers and the kinds of item in it](pathname:///img/diagrams/items-dark.svg)

Every item has short answers, such as **Agree** or **Change**, **Looks right**, **I confirm**, or **Fix it**. Your agent asks the same answers in the terminal, and the App records what you picked.

## Comments

Select any passage, line or part of a diagram and write a comment. Your agent answers in the thread and says what it changed. **Ask Anna about this** passes the question to a teammate.

## What it costs {#cost}

![The cost panel: the total, each step with its time, tokens and cost, and each agent named after its work](pathname:///img/diagrams/cost-light.svg)
![The cost panel: the total, each step with its time, tokens and cost, and each agent named after its work](pathname:///img/diagrams/cost-dark.svg)

**Cost** in a task's header shows the total and each step's time and cost, estimated at list price from your agents' own logs. Nothing is sent anywhere.

## In Claude Code

A slim band above your prompt says what your agent asks next. `/ql-pane` opens a pane with the steps and the build's progress.

## Handy to know

- **⌘K** finds a task or a setting.
- **Copy** on any document pastes it into Confluence, Notion, Jira or GitHub.
- The App notifies you only when you can act: a Plan or review waits, a task stops or ships, or a teammate asks you.
